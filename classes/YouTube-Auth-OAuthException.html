OAuthException.php : 29

Google's OAuth server refused a request.

getError() is the OAuth error code, which says what to do next: authorization_pending and slow_down while a device sign-in waits for its user, access_denied when they declined it, expired_token when they took too long, and invalid_grant when a refresh token has been revoked or has expired.
